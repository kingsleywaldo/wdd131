Overview

Goodies Bake Concepts — a bakery producing quality cakes, breads, pastries and desserts for every occasion. This document outlines the site purpose, target scenarios, color palette, typography, and wireframes for mobile and desktop.

Site Purpose

The website will showcase bakery products, enable customers to place orders or request custom cakes, display a gallery of work, share business information, and provide contact options including delivery details and pickup.

Key User Scenarios

  • Browse available cake types, sizes, and pricing.
  • Order a custom birthday or wedding cake with a consultation form.
  • Arrange home delivery or local pickup and check delivery zones.
  • Contact Goodies Bake Concepts for custom requests and event orders.

Color Palette

  • Chocolate Brown — #6F4E37 (headings, navigation, footer)
  • Cream — #FFF8F0 (page background)
  • Gold — #D4AF37 (buttons, accents)

Typography

  • Poppins — Headings
  • Arial — Body text (fallback)
