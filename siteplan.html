Goodies Bake Concepts Website Plan

Site Name

Goodies Bake Concepts

The name reflects our bakery business that creates quality cakes, bread, pastries and desserts for every occasion.

Site Purpose

This website will showcase our bakery products, allow customers to place orders, view our gallery, learn about our business and contact us for custom cake requests.

Scenarios

  • What types of cakes do you bake?
  • How can I order a custom birthday or wedding cake?
  • Do you offer home delivery?
  • How do I contact Goodies Bake Concepts?

Color Schema

  • Chocolate Brown (#6F4E37) - Headings, navigation and footer.
  • Cream (#FFF8F0) - Page background.
  • Gold (#D4AF37) - Buttons and highlights.

Typography

  • Poppins - Headings
  • Arial - Body text
