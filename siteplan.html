Goodies Bake Concepts Website Plan

Site Name

Goodies Bake Concepts

The name reflects our bakery business that creates quality cakes, bread, pastries, and desserts for every occasion. The name is simple, memorable, and represents our brand.

Site Purpose

This website will showcase Goodies Bake Concepts products and services. Customers will be able to view cakes, pastries, and other baked goods, learn about the business, place orders, and contact the bakery for custom requests.

Scenarios

  • What types of cakes do you bake for birthdays and weddings?
  • How can I place an order for a custom cake?
  • Do you offer delivery services?
  • How can I contact Goodies Bake Concepts?

Color Schema

  • Chocolate Brown (#6F4E37): Used for headings, navigation, and footer.
  • Cream (#FFF8F0): Used as the page background.
  • Gold (#D4AF37): Used for buttons, accents, and highlights.

Typography

  • Poppins: Used for headings and important titles.
  • Arial: Used for body text and paragraphs.
